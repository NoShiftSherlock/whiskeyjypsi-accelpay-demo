How this works

This is Whiskey JYPSI's own Shopify store with the AccelPay Shopify app installed. The cart and checkout are Shopify. AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing each order to a licensed retailer.

01Your Shopify storeYou keep ownership of the store, the domain, the theme and the checkout. No third-party cart and no redirect.
02Shopify checkoutThe one-page Shopify checkout with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and card payments by AccelPay.
03Licensed retailerThe shipping address picks a licensed retailer in the buyer's state.
04Your customer dataEvery order and customer record lands in Whiskey JYPSI's Shopify admin. You own the customer.
For the Whiskey JYPSI team

Same store, off Flaviar Checkout

Today whiskeyjypsi.com runs on Flaviar Checkout, where Flaviar takes ownership of the Shopify store and controls payments, shipping and order edits. With the AccelPay Shopify app the store stays in your account, and AccelPay handles payments, taxes and routing to licensed retailers.

  • 11 retailers in our network have Whiskey JYPSI in stock today, across Florida, Texas, California, New York, DC and Tennessee.
  • Ships to 40+ states through 3,500+ licensed retail locations, ordered through your distributor, so every DTC order counts toward depletions.
  • Use any app in the Shopify App Store, including Recharge for subscriptions and bundle builders to create unique collections of your products. Flaviar's gateway limits subscriptions.
  • Sell everything on one storefront: alcohol, non-alcoholic drinks, merch, hemp-based THC and tobacco.
  • Additional benefits are available to brands that use Southern Glazer's or Park Street as their distributor or importer.
  • Live in about one week from kickoff, with no per-order charge to the brand.

Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.

Reordering

Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
